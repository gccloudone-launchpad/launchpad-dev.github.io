## PIE

This is the short-term, high-power compute service that's often been talked about. Clients don't get a full cloud environment, but rather get shell access to a VM (and possibly storage/database if needed), for a limited amount of time to perform some amount of large-scape computing.
After the time is up, and they're done, the resources get destroyed.
This supports things like genomic processing, AI/ML training/processing, etc.., but only in cases where the clients know what they're doing, and just need more compute power than their laptops/departments can quickly provide.
Costing for this would be subsidized to start, as we figure out the true cost of running such a service, so wording around that would need to be provided in the pages to make it clear.

## Managed tenants

This one is where clients need access to EntraID with more/higher permissions than we can offer them with traditional environments. We'd handle provisioning the tenant, and setting up the guardrails. Once it's ready, we give them GA accounts, and they do their thing. We mitigate the tenant sprawl concern that Cloud Brokering might have the same way we do with other environments, by time-boxing it.

Advisory, PIE, and Managed Tenants are still very much in ideation stage (pre-pre-pre-alpha), but could be interesting to put on the website to gauge interest in them, and gather feedback about what they might look like. Call it market research :).
