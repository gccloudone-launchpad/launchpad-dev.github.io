Not sure if LaunchPad is the right fit? That's completely normal. Before you submit an intake request, it helps to spend a moment thinking through a few things. You don't need to have all the answers — but a little preparation can make the process smoother for everyone.

## Is LaunchPad right for you?

LaunchPad is designed for Government of Canada organizations that want to explore a specific idea, problem, or technology in a cloud environment — without the full overhead of standing up production infrastructure.

It's a good fit if you:

- Have a concrete problem you want to explore using cloud technology
- Are not yet ready (or able) to commit to a full production deployment and want to innovate in cloud
- Want access to expert guidance while you experiment
- Can dedicate team time to the experiment over a period of months

It may not be the right fit if you are looking for permanent production hosting, or if your workload involves data that exceeds the classification levels supported by the platform.

## What to think about before applying

Before submitting your intake request, it helps to have a general sense of:

- **What you want to test** — What is the problem you are trying to solve? What hypothesis are you working with?
- **Your data** — What classification level is the data involved? Are there any sensitivity or residency constraints?
- **Your team** — Who will be doing the work? Do you have someone comfortable working in a cloud environment, or will you need support getting up to speed?
- **Your timeline** — Do you have a rough idea of how long the experiment might take, and does that align with your organization's priorities?

## Skills and knowledge

You do not need to be a cloud expert to work with LaunchPad. However, experiments tend to go more smoothly when your team has:

- A technically curious mindset and willingness to learn
- At least one person comfortable navigating a cloud console or CLI
- A clear understanding of the problem you are trying to solve
- Awareness of any relevant IT or security policies within your department

If you are unsure whether your team has the right skills, reach out — we are happy to have that conversation before you apply.

## Helpful resources

Brushing up on some cloud fundamentals before you start can help your team hit the ground running. Some areas worth exploring:

- Cloud service models (IaaS, PaaS, SaaS)
- The cloud service providers available through LaunchPad: Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
- Your department's data classification and acceptable use policies
