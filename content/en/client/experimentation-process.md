Once your experiment is underway, here is what you can expect — how we work together, what our check-ins look like, and how we know when the work is done.

## What an experiment looks like

A typical LaunchPad experiment runs between **3 and 9 months**. During that time, your team works within a secure, managed cloud sandbox to build, test, and evaluate your idea. The pace and scope of the work is shaped by your goals and what you are learning along the way.

Experiments are intentionally flexible — the point is to explore, not to deliver a finished product. It is okay to pivot, adjust scope, or change direction based on what you discover.

## How we work together

You will be paired with a LaunchPad advisor who will support you throughout the experiment. Together, you will establish a working rhythm that fits your team's needs.

Regular check-ins typically include:

- Reviewing progress against your experiment goals
- Unblocking any technical, access, or process issues
- Discussing what you are learning and whether any adjustments make sense
- Ensuring your work stays within the boundaries of the platform's security and compliance requirements

The frequency of meetings is agreed upon at the start of the experiment and can be adjusted as needed.

## Staying on track

Your advisor is there to help — not to audit you. If something is not working, or if your hypothesis has changed, the sooner we know, the sooner we can adapt. Open communication throughout the experiment leads to better outcomes.

Partway through the experiment, we will do a more formal check-in to assess progress and confirm whether the experiment is still heading in the right direction.

## Knowing when you are done

An experiment is not necessarily about achieving a specific outcome — it is about generating enough evidence to make a decision. That decision might be:

- **Move forward** — The experiment validated the approach and you are ready to take next steps
- **Pivot** — You learned something that points in a different direction worth exploring
- **Conclude** — The experiment answered the question, and the answer is "not right now" or "not this way"

Your advisor will help you recognize when you have reached that point and support you in documenting what you found.
