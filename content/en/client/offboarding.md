Every experiment has an end. Offboarding is the process of wrapping up your work in LaunchPad and deciding where to go next. There are several paths forward — and the right one depends on what you learned and what your organization needs.

## Your options after an experiment

### Move to production

If your experiment was a success and you are ready to operationalize your workload, the next step is moving to a production environment. That could mean:

- **Departmental cloud** — Your organization takes ownership and moves the workload into its own cloud account or infrastructure
- **GCCO production** — In some cases, SSC may be able to support a transition to a GCCO-managed production environment

Either way, your LaunchPad advisor can help you understand what the transition involves and point you toward the right resources.

### Start another experiment

Still have questions? Discovered a new problem worth exploring? You may be eligible for a follow-on experiment. LaunchPad is designed to support iterative discovery — one experiment does not have to be the end of the journey.

Talk to your advisor about whether another experiment makes sense and how to apply.

### Share your results

Regardless of the outcome, what you learned has value. We encourage all clients to document and share their findings — whether through an internal report, a presentation to leadership, or a contribution to the broader GC cloud community.

Your advisor can help you think through how to structure and communicate what you discovered.

## Planning ahead

The path you have in mind after your experiment can influence how you design it from the start. If you already know you want to move to production, it is worth designing your experiment with that transition in mind — for example, by following practices that will carry over into a production environment.

Talk to your advisor early if you have a clear destination in mind. The earlier we know, the better we can help you get there.
