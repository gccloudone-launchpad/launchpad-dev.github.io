Ready to get started? Here is everything you need to know about applying to LaunchPad and what happens between submitting your request and launching your experiment.

## The front door

The first step is submitting an [intake request](https://ltpms-sgpel.science.cloud-nuage.canada.ca/app/scie-p-intake#/welcome/english). This is how we learn about your experiment and begin assessing whether LaunchPad is the right environment for it.

## What to include in your intake request

The more detail you provide, the better we can assess your request and match you with the right support. Plan to include:

- A clear description of what you want to experiment with
- The scope and goals of your experiment
- An estimated cost and timeframe, if known
- Your preferred cloud service provider, if you have one in mind (Microsoft Azure, AWS, or GCP)
- The area of innovation you want to explore (e.g. infrastructure, platform, data, AI, etc.)

## What happens after you apply

Once your request is received, we review it and aim to be in touch within **10 business days**. We do not automatically approve all experiments — each application is assessed for suitability based on the LaunchPad mandate and available capacity.

If your experiment is selected, SSC may cover cloud consumption costs up to $5,000. All experiments must comply with our [Terms and Conditions]({{< globallink "terms-and-conditions" >}}).

## From approval to launch

If your experiment is approved, your assigned LaunchPad advisor will reach out to:

- Confirm the scope and goals of your experiment
- Walk you through the environment and what access you will have
- Identify any onboarding steps specific to your team or workload
- Set up your initial meeting and establish a working cadence

Most teams are up and running shortly after this initial conversation.
